- Created by.

MODULE B — UPI PAYMENT PREPARATION

8. ADMIN UPI CONFIGURATION

Allow Admin to configure the tuition centre's UPI details.

Fields:

- UPI ID.
- Payee or tuition centre name.
- Optional payment description.
- Active/inactive status.
- Updated by.
- Updated timestamp.

Validate UPI ID format before saving.

Show the currently configured UPI ID clearly to students.

Do not expose any private credentials or payment secrets.

Do not store bank passwords, UPI PINs or other sensitive authentication information.

9. STUDENT PAY NOW EXPERIENCE

Create a Pay Now option inside the Student Fee section.

When the student chooses to pay:

1. Load the current outstanding balance.
2. Allow a partial payment or full outstanding payment.
3. Validate the requested amount.
4. Display the configured UPI ID.
5. Display the payee name.
6. Display the amount.
7. Show a clear payment summary.
8. Allow the student to continue to a compatible UPI app.

Where supported, prepare a standard UPI payment URI using the configured payee and requested amount.

The UPI URI must be generated from validated application data.

Do not hardcode the UPI ID or amount.

Do not claim that opening a UPI app means payment succeeded.

Payment preparation rules

- Amount must be greater than zero.
- Amount cannot exceed the outstanding balance.
- Prevent duplicate payment-request records from accidental repeated clicks.
- Use a unique payment reference for each request.
- Display a clear status after returning to the app.
- If no compatible UPI app is available, display the UPI ID and payment details for manual use.
- Do not collect the student's UPI PIN.
- Do not ask for banking credentials.
- Do not pretend that the application can read a bank transaction automatically.

10. PAYMENT SUBMISSION

After attempting payment through a UPI app, the student may submit a payment claim.

Fields:

- Payment reference ID.
- Student ID.
- Fee period.
- Amount claimed.
- UPI transaction reference or UTR, where available.
- Payment date.
- Optional student note.
- Submitted timestamp.
- Verification status.

Use a unique internal payment reference.

Clearly distinguish:

- Payment prepared.
- Payment claim submitted.
- Verification pending.
- Verified.
- Rejected.

A student-submitted claim is not a confirmed payment.

Do not mark the fee balance as paid when a student merely opens a UPI app or submits a payment claim.

MODULE C — MANUAL ADMIN PAYMENT VERIFICATION

11. ADMIN PAYMENT VERIFICATION DASHBOARD

Create an Admin Payment Verification section.

Display:

- Pending payment claims.
- Student name and ID.
- Claimed amount.
- Fee period.
- UTR or transaction reference.
- Payment date.
- Submission date.
- Current verification status.

Admin must be able to:

- Inspect a payment claim.
- Compare the claim with independently checked payment information.
- Verify a payment.
- Reject a payment.
- Add an internal verification note.
- View verification history.

Critical payment-security rule

A screenshot alone must never be sufficient proof of payment.

A screenshot may be supporting evidence, but Admin must independently confirm the transaction using an authorized source outside this application before marking it verified.

This phase does not provide automatic bank verification.

Do not invent a bank integration.

Do not claim that a transaction reference is genuine merely because it is entered by a student.

Verification requirements

- Only Admin can verify or reject payment claims.
- Verification requires an explicit confirmation.
- Rejection requires a reason.
- Verification must record the Admin actor and timestamp.
- Duplicate transaction references must be flagged for investigation.
- Prevent the same payment claim from being verified twice.
- Prevent verified payment records from being silently edited.
- Corrections require an audit entry and reason.

When verified:

1. Create a confirmed payment record.
2. Update the relevant fee-period balance.
3. Update the student's total paid amount.
4. Update the remaining balance.
5. Update payment status.
6. Create a receipt record.
7. Notify the student inside the app.

When rejected:

- Keep the payment out of confirmed collections.
- Preserve the rejected claim.
- Record the rejection reason.
- Notify the student inside the app.
- Allow a new payment claim where appropriate.

MODULE D — PARTIAL AND FULL PAYMENTS

12. PAYMENT CALCULATIONS

Support both partial and full fee payments.

For each fee period:

Remaining Balance = Fee Due − Verified Payments

Rules:

- Only verified payments reduce the balance.
- Pending claims do not reduce the balance.
- Rejected claims do not reduce the balance.
- Partial payments must remain visible in payment history.
- Multiple verified payments may contribute to one fee period.
- Prevent overpayment beyond the outstanding balance unless a clearly documented future policy is approved.
- Do not silently move payments between students or fee periods.

Display:

- Total fee.
- Verified amount paid.
- Pending claims.
- Remaining amount.
- Due date.
- Payment status.

Use INR formatting consistently.

Avoid floating-point calculation errors. Use integer paise or another safe currency representation internally, and format the result for display in rupees.
